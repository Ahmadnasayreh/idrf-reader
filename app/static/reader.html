How encasement is defined

Encasement means the tumour is in contact with more than 50% of the vessel circumference. A flattened vein with no visible lumen also counts. Contact with less than 50% of the circumference is not an IDRF (INRG imaging consensus, Brisse et al., Radiology 2011).
